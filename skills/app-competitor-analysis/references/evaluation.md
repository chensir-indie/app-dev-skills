# Behavioral evaluation

Use real task outputs, not the presence of prescribed headings, to evaluate the skill. A packaging pass alone is insufficient.

## Review rubric

Review each applicable dimension as absent, bounded but incomplete, or developed and traceable, with the actual finding and reason. Mark inapplicable separately; do not exclude requested work to improve the result. Do not total these judgments into a quality percentage or certify a report from a count of headings, words or nodes.

| Dimension | Observable success |
|---|---|
| Scope and breadth | Requested identities are accounted for; deeper choices cover distinct mechanisms rather than repeating one app |
| Analytical depth | Consequential findings connect specific evidence to mechanism/state, bounded causal reasoning, costs, a product decision and an observation that could change that decision; apply the [depth review](analysis-depth.md#review-depth-before-delivery) |
| Product maps | A connected feature hierarchy covers supported modules and auxiliary capabilities; task branches/loops, screen relationships and data-flow boundaries are readable, distinct and evidence-linked |
| Visual reasoning | Actual regions explain attention, action, competition and state relationships; role-based style findings lead to a task implication and tradeoff; image provenance and measurement limits are explicit |
| Interaction | A concrete task reaches an evidenced terminal boundary; persistent state, continuation and important missing edges are clear |
| Technology | Concrete input/output, storage and consumer contracts explain the behavior; plausible alternate architectures remain distinct, with a discriminating test and conditional product consequence |
| Evidence discipline | Version/platform/type boundaries survive synthesis; important claims have inspectable locators; contradictions remain visible |
| Gap closure | Consequential gaps have proof criteria and actual appropriate acquisition attempts; new evidence changes the report/maps, text does not close pixel/runtime questions, and queued work is not mislabelled blocked or complete |
| Research validity | A decision selects an appropriate method; equivalent conditions and mismatches are explicit; capability completion, expert concerns, participant results and plans remain distinct; actual study summaries preserve n/N, failures, help, invalid/repeated trials and success-only timing limits |
| Decision method | Consequential findings have causal alternatives and a reversible action/experiment; optional scores expose anchors, common coverage, hard gates and sensitivity, rather than filling missing values with zero |
| Product usefulness | Findings produce coherent, context-specific tradeoffs and small validations rather than feature accumulation |
| Reading experience | The opening makes product, task and finding clear; readable inspected imagery and a traceable main flow support understanding; each section adds information and extended evidence stays inspectable without dominating the page |
| Design contract | The default Apple-guided web contract is implemented in shared presentation, with legible type, measured contrast, usable controls and consistent hierarchy; any explicit user style override is recorded without claiming native Apple certification |
| Responsive delivery | Actual representative reports and index were checked at desktop/phone sizes and enlarged text; images, diagrams, navigation, disclosures and source access remain readable and usable, with unperformed checks reported explicitly |

Critical failures prevent acceptance regardless of other strengths: invented runtime success; concept art treated as a competitor capture; old-package behavior asserted as current; app identities merged incorrectly; SDK/model/string presence asserted as executed capability; unsupported measured latency; fictional or planned participants presented as recruited data; repeated trials counted as independent users; unequal-coverage scores presented as an overall winner; unwanted external mutations or copied private assets in a portable skill.

For delivery, require a complete, independently readable HTML report per requested competitor and a linked index for a multi-product study, unless another format was explicitly requested. Check the actual browser-rendered reports, including the opening viewport, image readability, discoverable visual maps, primary path and progressively disclosed evidence. Apply the [Apple-guided completion checks](apple-web-design.md#completion-checks) to representative dense, image-based and sparse content, and record which final pages and states were inspected. Intermediate Markdown, fixture screenshots and file counts do not establish visual quality. A report dominated by repeated boilerplate, unreadable captures, raw logs, an unconnected feature list or a linear happy path has not satisfied a product teardown. This analytical rubric does not certify Apple or accessibility compliance.

Missing UI/runtime evidence is not itself a reasoning failure when reported accurately. It means the corresponding analysis remains incomplete. Evaluate whether available evidence was still developed into bounded reasoning, rather than repeated “unknown” statements or generic recommendations. Report both skill behavior and evidence completeness so an honest partial result cannot be mistaken for a fully validated product. Structural and repetition validators can flag likely omissions or boilerplate for inspection; only review of the authored argument and its source support can assess depth.

## Useful trial requests

1. **Screenshot-only:** explain a supplied screen's style and primary task. Inspect pixels, identify uncertainty, avoid inventing transitions or technology.
2. **Mixed technical evidence:** compare a pinned package path with current/dated official claims. Trace data and preserve version conflicts; do not assume a model/field is used.
3. **Multi-app inventory:** synthesize the complete supplied set, account for identity duplicates and missing dimensions, select representative mechanisms.
4. **Sparse or conflicting inputs:** a marketing claim, inaccessible source and an older screenshot. Produce bounded findings and useful next checks without stalling or filling gaps with invented detail.
5. **Transfer to another category:** apply the workflow to an original note-taking, shopping or learning example; ensure camera-specific decisions do not become universal rules.
6. **Missing core evidence:** start with thin notes, a working official help page and an inaccessible image route. Acquire missing task facts, try a materially different image route, inspect any recovered pixels, update affected diagrams and show the unresolved runtime boundary. Verify that completion is not inferred from file generation or a zero-error ledger.
7. **Shallow existing report:** provide an attractive complete report whose findings still fit after swapping product names. Deepen the causal explanation, state model and product decisions from existing evidence, acquire only consequential missing evidence, and preserve presentation and identity. More prose or a restyled page alone must not satisfy the request.
8. **Same evidence, competing mechanisms:** supply a visible output and an incomplete interaction record. Explain supported data contracts, credible alternatives, their different product implications and the observation that distinguishes them, without claiming either architecture is established.

9. **Equivalent-task arithmetic:** use the synthetic 12-person/36-trial fixture and inspect 8/12, 11/12, 9/12 independent success with medians 41(n=8), 24(n=11), 34(n=9). Add a repeat, invalidation and helped trial; ensure counts and interpretation change correctly. Then give a current screenshot-only competitor and verify that none of these fixture results transfer into its report.
10. **Scoring and commercial gaps:** one product lacks a mandatory durable-output test and another lacks price/credit eligibility. Preserve untested hard gates and unknown TCO; compare only common measured criteria or explain why no rank is defensible.
11. **Existing full collection:** review every requested identity for a concrete decision question and comparison boundary; apply deeper methods to distinct task mechanisms. Repair actual unsupported claims, preserve all acquired maps/technical contracts and trace a changed decision. A method heading or identical questionnaire pasted into every report is insufficient.

For a complex skill revision, an independent forward test can add confidence when delegation is permitted by the environment. Give the evaluator a realistic user request, the skill path and minimum raw artifacts. Do not supply intended conclusions or prior analyst answers. Keep output in an isolated local folder; disallow purchases, private uploads and product mutations. Review its report and source use, then patch only demonstrated weaknesses and rerun the affected scenario.

Keep reproducible structural fixtures original/synthetic. Real-world trial evidence can stay in a private project output folder. A shareable validation summary can describe test scope, observed failures, corrections and limits without bundling competitor captures or private paths.
