## Observable changes

- A brief now selects a decision, alternative, falsifiable question, method and comparable subgroup before dimensions are expanded. Direct/indirect/substitute products remain in a full requested inventory.
- Research records separate declared capabilities from observed task completion, source-based concerns from observed incidents, and plans from actual participants/telemetry. Equivalent-task protocols define neutral prompts, correct terminal outcomes, conditions, order, timeout/help/error/exclusion rules and recording locators.
- `study_metrics.py` preserves independent/helped/failure outcomes, invalidations, repetitions, participant denominators, success-only time subsets, all-attempt medians and matched participant IDs. It checks arithmetic and record consistency, not recruitment or causal validity.
- Heuristic severity uses frequency/impact/persistence with explicit scale provenance. Market/review/cohort and TCO methods preserve scope and unknowns. Scoring requires observable anchors, common coverage, hard gates and sensitivity; complete fictional examples demonstrate refusing an invalid global rank.
- Findings lead to a reversible decision and an experiment with control, outcome, guardrails and stopping interpretation. Blank records and heading counts cannot establish analytical quality.

## Scenario review and limits

Manual scenario review (not blind model evaluation): a narrow technical request preserves that endpoint; a full collection retains every identity; an existing shallow report is deepened without discarding maps; missing participant/device access leaves methods not_run while source research continues; missing Python has a read-only doctor and documented repair. Unknown prices do not become zero TCO, unequal coverage does not produce an overall score, and a small exploratory protocol does not become a statistically validated winner.

No competitor device runtime, recruited-user study, payment, long-term cohort or production experiment was executed. Unresolved acquisition questions stay unresolved. Browser performance and automated tests validate their named implementation boundaries; they do not certify professional research quality.
